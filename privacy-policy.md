# Privacy Policy - SipMate Discord Bot

**Last Updated:** [DATE]

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how SipMate Discord Bot ("we", "our", "us", "the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our hydration tracking service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Automatically Collected Information
- **Discord User ID**: Unique identifier provided by Discord for progress tracking
- **Discord Server ID**: Server identifiers where the Bot is installed
- **Command Usage Data**: Which commands you use and when (for functionality)
- **Timestamps**: When you interact with the Bot

### 2.2 Information You Provide
- **Custom Reset Times**: Personal schedule preferences you set
- **Server Configuration**: Channel settings for automatic reminders
- **Interaction Data**: Your responses to Bot commands

### 2.3 Information We Do Not Collect
- We do not collect or store message content beyond commands directed to the Bot
- We do not access your personal messages or conversations
- We do not collect personal identifying information (name, email, phone)
- We do not track your activity outside of Bot interactions

### 3.1 Primary Uses
- **Hydration Tracking**: Calculate and display your water intake progress

### 3.2 Service Improvement

- **Bug Fixes**: Identify and resolve technical issues

### 3.3 Communication
- **Automated Reminders**: Send hydration reminders as configured
